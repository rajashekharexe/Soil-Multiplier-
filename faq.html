🌱 About the Product
What is KAD Multiplier?
KAD Multiplier is an organic soil microbiome booster containing 47 beneficial microbial strains. It improves soil health, enhances crop yields, and promotes sustainable farming by restoring natural soil biology.
How does KAD Multiplier work?
It works by introducing beneficial microorganisms into the soil that break down organic matter, fix nitrogen, solubilize phosphorus, and suppress harmful pathogens — creating ideal conditions for plant growth.
Is it safe for all crops?
Yes. KAD Multiplier is suitable for all crops including vegetables, fruits, cereals, and cash crops. It is 100% organic with no harmful chemicals.
What is the shelf life of the product?
The product has a shelf life of 12 months from the date of manufacture when stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Check the label for the exact expiry date.
How should I store KAD Multiplier?
Store in a cool, dry place at room temperature (below 35°C). Avoid direct sunlight. Do not store near pesticides or chemicals.
