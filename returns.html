⚠️ We do not accept returns for products that have been opened or used, or for change-of-mind.

💰 Refund Policy

Refunds are processed under these conditions:

⏱️ Please allow 5–7 additional days for the refund to reflect in your account depending on your bank.

❌ Non-Returnable Situations
